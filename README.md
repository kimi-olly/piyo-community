## About

### Communities are hard to start

Starting and running a community is hard for beginners. Few people help, and roles are unclear. Piyo makes it simple and fun.

- Beginners often don't know how to set up rules
- It is hard to keep members helping
- Piyo is a DAO app that runs a community for you
- Contribute, and your chick grows

### How Piyo works

Pick a template, answer three questions, and start. Then small steps grow your chick and raise your role.

- Pick a template: hobby circle, local, or fans & learning
- Answer 3 questions: purpose, size, and money
- See a similar example and start with that form
- Home: small tasks, invites, and a shared goal on one screen

### Why it matters and what's next

Piyo makes helping feel like a game. Roles change by themselves, so leaders do less manual work.

- Green, Duolingo-style design with a chick, streaks, and an XP bar
- Falling back is gentle: you can always return to Chick
- Japanese and English with one switch
- Next: decide how many growth stages to use
